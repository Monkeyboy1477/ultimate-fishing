Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

No account, no personal data collected by us

Ultimate Fishing doesn't require an account and doesn't ask you for any personal information. Your progress, gear, and catches are saved only in your own browser's local storage, on your own device — we never see it and it never leaves your browser.

Ads and cookies

This site shows ads through Google AdSense to help keep the game free. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You'll see a banner the first time you visit asking whether you consent to this; declining means no ad cookies are set and no ads are shown.

You can learn more about how Google uses this information, and opt out of personalized advertising generally, at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and adssettings.google.com.

Children's privacy

This game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change too.

Questions

Open an issue on the GitHub repository and we'll get back to you.
